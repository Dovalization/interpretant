How to judge
A use
The marked place is the target word in one of its parts of speech (any part of speech when it is part of a hyphenated word), in any sense, inflection or spelling, and not inside a name (a person, organisation, place, product, event, law, named test or scale, or a title). Mentioning the word as a word counts.
Kept
Right if the marked place is a use and the mark covers exactly the word or part.
Left out
Right if leaving it out was correct: wrong part of speech, or inside a name.
Missed
Right if it is a use the program should have kept; wrong if it is another word, a part of speech or name that would be left out, or OCR damage.
