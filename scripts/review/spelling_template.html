Are these two spellings of one word?

The word lists will write the left form as the right one, because they look like two spellings of the same word (British and American, -ise and -ize, -our and -or). Say whether that is right for the word.

  • Same word when the two are spellings of one word, even if the sentences show different senses of it: both spellings carry every sense (economic and optical polarisation).
  • Not the same when the spelling itself marks a different word or sense (analyses, the plural noun, against analyzes, the verb), when one is not English or is a misspelling of another word, or when the right-hand form is not a real spelling.
  • Names and other languages are handled by a rule. If a sentence shows the word only as a name or in another language, judge the English word.

Which side is the standard was set by how often the corpus uses each, so don't judge that. Keys: 1 same word, 2 not the same, 3 not sure; arrows move.
